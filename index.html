Rules

  • Play until rested XP runs out, park in an inn, switch.
  • Dungeons only when fully rested (rested doubles kill XP).
  • Mail materials around so every profession keeps climbing.
  • One character per class: level points count once per class.
  • Only crafting professions earn points (150 / 225 / 300).

Route

Phase 1: Supply chain Priest 1–5 → Druid 1–15 → Shaman 1–10 → Warrior 1–10 → Priest 5–10

Done when: Druid has Alchemy in Stormwind, everyone else is 10 with bags.

1. Priest 1–5

  • Train Tailoring + Enchanting in Darnassus. Park.

2. Druid 1–15

  • Herbalism at 5: Halassa Fernbreeze, Shen'dar Village /way 43.0 43.6. Bank every herb.
  • Bear Form quests from Lotheluum Starbreeze.
  • Park at the Calmbreeze Inn between sessions.
  • At ~15: skycutter south of Valanaar → Dalaran landing (grab Child of Nature) → portal to Stormwind.
  • Stormwind: train Alchemy, turn in Child of Nature (Teleport: Moonglade). Mail potions out. Park.

3. Shaman 1–10

  • Skinning (~3), The Great Outdoors for Camping (~5), Leatherworking in Ironforge.
  • Leatherworking 20: Camping 101 → Camp Tent.
  • Class quests: Call of Earth, Call of Fire at 10.
  • Mail Linen to the Priest. Grab A Visitor to Dun Morogh (Beldin Steelgrill). Park.

4. Warrior 1–10

  • Mining: Fanorran Stilloak, Shadowglen /way 58.2 41.6.
  • Class quest: Defensive Stance at 10 (Darnassus).
  • Mail Linen and greens to the Priest. Park.

5. Priest 5–10

  • Make 16 Linen Bags: 4 each for Druid, Shaman, Warrior and yourself.
  • Disenchant the greens. Class quest: Stars of Elune at 10 (Dolanaar). Park.
  • Optional: create the support alts and park them.
Phase 2: First gauntlet Levels 13–21

Done when: core four are 20–21 and one craft is at 150 (unlocks Legacy).

Night Elves (Priest + Warrior) at ~11

  • Rut'theran → fly to Auberdine. Warrior: Blacksmithing + Journeyman Mining.
  • Darkshore to 13. Grab Researching the Corruption (for BFD).
  • Boat to Stormwind, set hearth, tram to Ironforge.

Pick up before running

  • Hall of Thanes: A Visitor to Dun Morogh, Old Ironforge Incursion chain (Farsen /way 64.8 58.5).
  • Deadmines: Westfall chains at Sentinel Hill, Stormwind quests. Bring 10 Rough Dynamite.
  • Wailing Caverns: Ratchet and entrance quests.
  • Level 14: Cozy Sleeping Bag chain, Westfall /way 37 50. Lets you park anywhere.

Run rested

  • Hall of Thanes → Deadmines → Wailing Caverns.
  • Push Tailoring or Leatherworking to 150 first: it unlocks Legacy early. First points into Working Overtime.
Phase 3: Stockade + Blackfathom Levels 21–25

Done when: core four are 25 (about 9 points).

Route

  • Quest Darkshore → Redridge → Duskwood → Ashenvale.
  • Run the Stockade, then Blackfathom Deeps.
  • Grab the Gnomeregan quests at 20.

Class quests at 20

  • Shaman: Call of Water.
  • Priest: Elune's Grace (Stormwind Cathedral).
  • Warrior: Fire Hardened Armor (Lakeshire → Stormwind).
  • Druid: Cure Poison and Cat Form (Moonglade, Darkshore).
Phase 4: Support alts + Excavation Site Core 25–29 · Support 1–14

Done when: support alts are 14 with Phase 2 quests; core four have the Excavation Site quests.

Support alts

  • Level to 14 with starting-zone class quests. Pick up the Phase 2 dungeon quests.

Core four

  • Ashenvale → Thousand Needles. Grab Seeking Caitlin in Astranaar.
  • Excavation Site (Wetlands): quests from Lakeshire, Menethil and Ironforge.
  • Warrior: Fire Hardened Armor spider fangs, Wetlands /way 46 61.
  • Shaman at 30: Call of Air (Arathi Highlands).
Phase 5: Push to 35 Core 28–35 · Support 14–25

Done when: core four are 35, support alts are 25, about 16 points.

Core four

  • City of Dalaran → Gnomeregan → Razorfen Kraul → Scarlet Monastery.
  • Professions to 225 (+1 point each).

Support alts

  • Hall of Thanes → Deadmines → Wailing Caverns, then Stockade + BFD to 25.
  • Hunter: Engineering to 150 with the Warrior's bars.

Legacy Point priority

  1. Early: Working Overtime. Faster profession leveling, so profession points arrive sooner.
  2. After the profession push: Well Rested with every point not in Working Overtime.
Core four16 points

Working Overtime 5, Well Rested 5, Talented 5, last 1 anywhere.

Alts 5–916 points

Well Rested 5, Talented 5, then Bountiful Harvest 5 (gatherers) or Thrill of Adventure 5 (zuggers), last 1 anywhere.

How many points

  • 18–19 once the core four and alts 5–9 are 25. More with extra profession leveling.
  • 16 spendable at launch (Blizzard). No date for raising the cap.
  • 45+ long term from leveling to 60 and professions.
  • 55 for the free epic mount on all characters: about 10 more beyond that.

Check at launch

  • Novice Spelunker dungeon list unpublished; may need Ragefire Chasm.
