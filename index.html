Route

Tap a step to tick it.
Phase 1: Supplies and bags Shaman → Priest → Warrior → Warlock, each 1–10

Done when: core four are 10 with bags, parked.

1. Shaman (Dun Morogh)

  • Skinning + Mining. Mail copper to the Warrior.
  • Leaving Coldridge: drop Mining for Leatherworking. Camp Tent at Leatherworking 20.
  • Mail Linen for tailoring, greens for disenchanting and 5 silver to the Priest.

2. Priest (Teldrassil)

  • Enchanting + Tailoring.
  • Linen Bags: keep 4, mail the rest to the Shaman, Warrior and Warlock.
  • Disenchant every green.

3. Warrior (Teldrassil)

  • Mining + Blacksmithing. Bank spare bars for Engineering.
  • Mail Linen for tailoring and greens for disenchanting to the Priest.

4. Warlock (Northshire)

  • Herbalism + Alchemy. Mail potions to every alt and Linen for tailoring to the Priest.
  • Elwynn east in one pass: the lake, Guard Thomas's bridge, the logging camp, Stone Cairn Lake. Turn in when your bags are full of finished quests. Skip the tiny new quests.
  • Class quests: Imp, Voidwalker.
  • Optional: create the support alts and park them.
Phase 2: Prep Each 10–14

Done when: core four are 14 with the Hall of Thanes quests, parked.

Shaman at 10

  • Dun Morogh, then lower Loch Modan, to 14.

Night Elves (Priest + Warrior) at 10

  • Dolanaar and the western chains to 12, then Darkshore to 14. Ferry back to Teldrassil for turn-ins.
  • Boat from Auberdine to Stormwind Harbor, tram to Ironforge.

Warlock at 10

  • Westbrook and Hogger last, out of Elwynn at 11–12.
  • Westfall to 14: The People's Militia parts 1–2. Tram to Ironforge.

Core four

  • Dun Morogh: grab the Hall of Thanes quests below.
  • Cozy Sleeping Bag from 14: ~3% bonus XP for 2 hours.

Grab the quests

Hall of Thanes

  • Old Ironforge Incursion · Earthseer Farsen, Gol'Bolar QuarryFirst: Farsen's Watch, then loot the Dark Iron Map from Dark Iron Spies (Ironband's Compound) and turn in Underground Map.Lead-in: A Visitor to Dun Morogh (Beldin Steelgrill, Kharanos).
  • The Restless Dead · Afadra Dunwall, Old Ironforge entrance
  • Important Heirlooms · Thom Filch, Old Ironforge entrance

Inside: An Ancient Grudge, The Treaty of Understanding.

Phase 3: Hall of Thanes + Defias chain Each 14–18

Done when: core four are 18 with every Deadmines quest, parked.

Core four, rested

  • Hall of Thanes: about a level and a half, to 16.
  • Westfall: finish The People's Militia, then the Defias chain to the escort: 18. Fill with Darkshore, not Redridge.
  • Boats: Stormwind Harbor ↔ Auberdine. Night Elves skip the Wetlands run.

Phase 4: Deadmines Each 18–20

Done when: core four are 20, parked, and one craft is at 150.

Core four, rested

  • Deadmines with every quest. With a full group, farm Miners' Union Cards.
  • Keep VanCleef's Unsent Letter for the Stockade.
  • Stormwind book deliveries, then Redridge (Yowler, murlocs) to 20. Night Elves can finish Darkshore instead.
  • Tailoring or Leatherworking to 150 first: your first Legacy Point.

Phase 7: Support alts + Dalaran trip Core 25–29 · Support 1–14

Done when: support alts are 14; core four have run Ruins of Lordaeron and Shadowfang Keep and have the Excavation Site quests.

Support alts

  • Phases 1–2, to 14.
  • Skyborne Druid + Hunter: Zephras Isle to 14–15, skycutter to Dalaran, Skyborne Portal to Stormwind, then Westfall. At 15, Ruins of Lordaeron is close to Dalaran.
  • Druid or Hunter: Mining + Herbalism. Mail bars to the Warrior and herbs to the Warlock.

Core four

  • Ashenvale → Thousand Needles → Wetlands.
  • Boats: Menethil → Southshore → Auberdine, one way. Take it to Southshore, then go up to Dalaran. Use the Stormwind portal instead if it's live.
  • Ruins of Lordaeron: Novice Spelunker needs it. Its quests are inside (Captain Truman).
  • Shadowfang Keep: no Alliance quests besides class quests. Run it for the kill.
  • Shaman at 30: Call of Air (Eldrun Stormbreaker → Veyric Thunderhame, Arathi).

Done when: core four are 35, support alts are 25 (22 points with Novice Spelunker).

  • Experienced Spelunker counts each Scarlet wing. It also needs Krol'dok Stronghold (Riverglades, 38+), past this route.
  • Finish any profession short of 225.

Legacy Point priority

  1. Early: Working Overtime. Faster profession leveling.
  2. After the profession push: Well Rested.
Core four16 points

Working Overtime 5, Well Rested 5, Talented 5, last 1 anywhere.

Alts 5–916 points

Well Rested 5, Talented 5, then Bountiful Harvest 5 (gatherers) or Thrill of Adventure 5 (zuggers), last 1 anywhere.

How many points

  • 21 once all nine classes are 25 and the six crafts are 225.
  • +1 for Novice Spelunker: the final boss of Hall of Thanes, Deadmines, Wailing Caverns, Ruins of Lordaeron, Shadowfang Keep and Excavation Site.
  • +1 for Experienced Spelunker: City of Dalaran, Blackfathom, Gnomeregan, Razorfen Kraul, four Scarlet wings, Drowned City, Krol'dok.
  • 16 spendable at launch.
  • 45+ long term, from 60s and professions.
  • 55 for the Spectral Bear mount.
