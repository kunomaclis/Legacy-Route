Alliance Legacy Point Route

Level a Main and eight alts together, and organize their professions so the whole army earns as many Legacy Points as possible. Rotate between characters so each one always plays with rested XP. The Horde dungeons from the original guide are replaced with ones near Stormwind and Ironforge, and the roster uses nine different classes so every level-25 point counts.

Army tracker

Enter levels and crafting skill to keep a live Legacy Point count. Tap Park when you log a character out in an inn, city or Cozy Sleeping Bag, and the tracker estimates their rested XP so you know who to play next. Everything saves on this device; use More → Export to move it to another device.

Rested estimate uses the Classic rule (5% of a level per 8 hours in an inn, capped at 1.5 levels) and assumes rested XP was empty when you parked. Treat it as a guide, not an exact number.

How the rotation works

  1. Play one character until its rested XP runs out.
  2. Park it in an inn or a city. It starts building rested XP again.
  3. Switch to the next character, which has been resting while you played the others.
  4. Save dungeons for when a character is fully rested. Rested XP doubles kill XP, and dungeons are mostly kills.
  5. Mail materials around as you go, so every character's profession keeps climbing.

Start order

Alchemy goes online first because potions and elixirs speed up everyone else. The Priest trains early, waits for Linen, then makes every bag in one sitting.

  1. Priest to 5: train Tailoring and Enchanting in Darnassus and park there.
  2. Druid to about 15: Herbalism on Zephras Isle, then Alchemy in Stormwind. This gets potions flowing as early as possible.
  3. Shaman to 10: Skinning and Leatherworking. Mail all Linen to the Priest.
  4. Warrior to 10: Apprentice Mining. Mail all Linen and greens to the Priest.
  5. Priest back on: turn the army's Linen into bags for everyone, disenchant the greens, then level 5 to 10.
Night Elf trip to the Eastern Kingdoms
  1. Leave Teldrassil at about level 11: take the portal down to Rut'theran Village and fly to Auberdine.
  2. Warrior: learn Blacksmithing in Auberdine, and Journeyman Mining from Kurdram Stonehammer (/way 38.2 41.0). Darkshore copper and tin feed Rough Sharpening Stones to 45, then Rough Grinding Stones to 83.
  3. Quest in Darkshore to 13 or 14. Pick up Researching the Corruption from Gershala Nightwhisper in Auberdine for Blackfathom Deeps later.
  4. Take the new direct boat from Auberdine to Stormwind Harbor, then set your hearthstone in Stormwind.
  5. Take the Deeprun Tram to Ironforge for Hall of Thanes. From then on the Night Elves follow the same route as everyone else.

The route

Open one phase at a time. Tap any step to tick it off, and each phase shows its progress. Tap a gold waypoint like /way 43.0 43.6 to copy it.

Phase 1: Set up the supply chain Priest 1 to 5, Druid 1 to 15, Shaman and Warrior 1 to 10, then Priest 5 to 10
GoalGet Alchemy running as early as possible, and have the Priest turn everyone's Linen into bags.
You're done whenThe Druid is in Stormwind with Alchemy. Shaman, Warrior and Priest are 10. Every core character has its bags. Everyone is parked.

1. Night Elf Priest to 5 (Teldrassil)

  • Level to 5 in Shadowglen. Trainers usually want level 5; if one teaches you at 4, take it.
  • Run to Darnassus and train Tailoring and Enchanting on the Craftsmen's Terrace.
  • Park in Darnassus. The Priest comes back once the others have Linen to send.

2. Skyborne Druid to about 15 (Zephras Isle, then Stormwind)

  • At 5, learn Herbalism from Halassa Fernbreeze in Shen'dar Village (/way 43.0 43.6). Pick every herb you pass and keep them for Alchemy.
  • Class quests on the isle: A Student of Nature at 2 (Rorian to Xyton Silverwind in Thendal Grove), then The Great Ursera Spirit and Strength and Mercy from Lotheluum Starbreeze for Bear Form.
  • Park at the Calmbreeze Inn in Shen'dar Village whenever you swap characters.
  • At about 15, take the skycutter from the tower south of Valanaar to the Dalaran landing. Pick up Child of Nature from Alfina Nightgaze, then take the Skyborne Portal to Stormwind.
  • In Stormwind: learn Alchemy and hand Child of Nature to Sheldras Moontree for Teleport: Moonglade. Make Elixir of Minor Defense with your stored herbs to reach 75, then mail potions and elixirs to the army.
  • Park in Stormwind.

3. Dwarf Shaman to 10 (Dun Morogh)

  • Class quests: Archaic Rune (Sten Stoutarm to Teo Hammerstorm in Anvilmar), then Call of Earth from Teo Hammerstorm. At 10, Call of Fire from Ingrid Dunwald to Bruegs Kindleborn at the South Gate.
  • Take Skinning from the free gathering manual quest around level 3 or 4.
  • Around level 5, do The Great Outdoors. It unlocks Camping.
  • Train Leatherworking in Ironforge. Make Light Armor Kits from your own Light Leather to reach 55.
  • At Leatherworking 20, the Camping 101 quest teaches the Camp Tent (5 Light Leather each), a rested-XP camp object.
  • Mail all Linen Cloth to the Priest.
  • Pick up A Visitor to Dun Morogh from Beldin Steelgrill at Steelgrill's Depot. It starts the Hall of Thanes chain.
  • Park in the Kharanos inn or in Ironforge.

4. Night Elf Warrior to 10 (Teldrassil)

  • Class quest at 10: the Night Elf Defensive Stance quest from the warrior trainer in Darnassus.
  • Learn Apprentice Mining from Fanorran Stilloak in Shadowglen (/way 58.2 41.6). There's no copper on Teldrassil, so mining starts in Darkshore.
  • Mail all Linen Cloth and every green to the Priest.
  • Park in Dolanaar. Blacksmithing comes in Auberdine at the start of Phase 2.

5. Night Elf Priest, 5 to 10 (bags for everyone)

  • Collect the Linen from the mailbox. Make 4 Linen Bags each for the Druid, Shaman and Warrior, mail them out, and keep 4 for yourself (16 in total). Make more for the support alts later.
  • Disenchant every green the army mailed you. Enchanting: Inferior Stamina bracers to 25, then Minor Deflect bracers to 90.
  • Class quest at 10: Stars of Elune, from Laurna Morninglight in Dolanaar.
  • Park in the Dolanaar inn or in Darnassus.
  • Optional: create the five support alts now and park them, so they start building rested XP.
Phase 2: First dungeon gauntlet Levels 13 to 21
GoalRun Hall of Thanes, Deadmines and Wailing Caverns on each core character while rested.
You're done whenAll four core characters are 20 or 21. One crafting profession has reached 150, which unlocks Legacy.

Night Elves only: getting to the Eastern Kingdoms

  • Follow the Night Elf trip under Start order.

Quests to pick up first, at level 13 or 14 (Ironforge and Dun Morogh)

  • Night Elves and the Druid: pick up A Visitor to Dun Morogh from Beldin Steelgrill at Steelgrill's Depot.
  • Old Ironforge Incursion chain: start with Farsen above the quarry (/way 64.8 58.5). Nip 'Em in the Bud (10 Dark Iron Spies), then Dark Iron Map, then Underground Map, then Old Ironforge Incursion (kill the final boss).
  • Important Heirlooms from Thom Filch (Old Ironforge, /way 32.6 44.6) and The Restless Dead from Afadra Dunwall.
  • Found inside: An Ancient Grudge and The Treaty of Understanding (can't be shared).
  • Knowledge in the Deeps from Gerrig Bonegrip in the Forlorn Cave. Keep it for Blackfathom Deeps later.
  • Park in Ironforge, next to the Hall of Thanes door.

Hall of Thanes (levels 13 to 18, 5 quests)

  • Run it fully rested and turn in every quest before moving on. The Druid is already about 15, which is still inside the range.

Westfall and Stormwind, before Deadmines

  • Destruction in Deadmines chain from Alba Fairmoon at Sentinel Hill (from level 9). Step 5 needs 10 Rough Dynamite, so buy or craft it in advance.
  • The Defias Brotherhood chain from Gryan Stoutmantle, and Red Silk Bandanas from Scout Riell.
  • In Stormwind: Collecting Memories and Oh Brother from Wilder Thistlenettle (the mine outside the instance is all elites, so bring the group), and Underground Assault from Gnoarn in Tinker Town.
  • Level 14: start the Cozy Sleeping Bag chain at the Burned-Out Remains, Alexston Farmstead, Westfall (/way 37 50). The bag works like an inn anywhere, even while you're logged out, so any character can park anywhere.

Deadmines (levels 14 to 24, 7 quests)

  • Drops the Journeyman Campfire and Cookie's Feast camp blueprints.
  • The Unsent Letter starts the Stockade Riots chain for Phase 3.

Wailing Caverns (levels 15 to 24, 5 Alliance quests)

  • Getting there: take the boat from Stormwind to Auberdine, then run south through Ashenvale into the Barrens. Or, once you have the Booty Bay flight path, take the Booty Bay to Ratchet boat. The Druid can use Teleport: Moonglade and fly to Auberdine.
  • Ratchet: Raptor Horns, then Smart Drinks (Mebok Mizzyrix), and Trouble at the Docks.
  • Above the entrance: Deviate Hides and Deviate Eradication. The Glowing Shard drops inside.
  • Lots of leather here for the Shaman to skin.

Optional: Ruins of Lordaeron (levels 16 to 22)

  • 4 Alliance quests, all inside, about 35,000 XP in total. Drops the Fermenter blueprint (the next Alchemy camp object).
  • Only worth it with a group already there. The walk is a boat from Menethil to Southshore, then north through Silverpine.

Professions during this phase

  • Get Leatherworking or Tailoring to 150 first. That unlocks Legacy before anyone reaches 25. Spend the first point on Working Overtime.
  • The Druid keeps herbing along the route and gets Alchemy to 150.
  • Skinning can only be done once per kill now, not once per group member. The Shaman should skin everything.
Phase 3: Stockade, Blackfathom Deeps, and the first Legacy points Levels 21 to 25
GoalReplace the Horde guide's Shadowfang Keep step with Alliance dungeons that have quests, and reach 25 on the core four.
You're done whenAll four core characters are 25. You have about 9 Legacy Points.

Why Shadowfang Keep is out

  • Its quests are Horde-only, except one Paladin quest. The Stockade replaces it. Blackfathom Deeps does the same job as in the original guide.

Questing between dungeons

  • Darkshore (16 to 21), then Redridge (20 to 23), then Duskwood (22 to 26), then Ashenvale (25 to 29).

Class quests that fit this stretch

  • Shaman, Call of Water (20 to 23): Eldrun Stormbreaker (Ironforge) to Norric Lochthane (Loch Modan) to Hervdana Saegrund (Wetlands). Fill waterskins below her cave, at Stonewatch Falls in Redridge and at the Astranaar lake in Ashenvale, then finish at Stendel's Pond in Westfall.
  • Priest, Elune's Grace (20): pick it up in Stormwind Cathedral during a Stormwind stop.
  • Warrior, Fire Hardened Armor (20): the Darnassus or Stormwind warrior trainer sends you to Yorus Barleybrew at the Lakeshire inn (timed run into the Rethban Caverns), then Furen Longbeard in Stormwind. The Wetlands spider fangs come in Phase 4.
  • Druid, while questing in Darkshore: Cure Poison (Mathrengyl Bearwalker in Darnassus, then Moonglade, then Cliffspring Falls) and, at 20, Cat Form (Darnassus, then Moonglade, then the Stormrage Barrow Dens). Take the Auberdine boat if you don't have the Rut'theran flight path.

The Stockade (levels 22 to 30, 6 quests)

  • Stormwind: Quell the Uprising and The Color of Blood.
  • Along the route: What Comes Around (Guard Berton, Lakeshire), Crime and Punishment (Councilman Millstipe, Darkshire), The Fury Runs Deep (Motley Garmason, Dun Modr, after The Dark Iron War), and The Stockade Riots (from the Deadmines letter).
  • Every quest giver is on your Redridge and Duskwood route, so there's no extra travel.

Blackfathom Deeps (levels 21 to 30), then on to 25

  • Quests: Knowledge in the Deeps (already in your log), Researching the Corruption (Auberdine), In Search of Thaelrid and Twilight Falls (Craftsmen's Terrace, Darnassus), and Blackfathom Villainy (inside).
  • Finish Ashenvale to reach 25.

Optional: Shadowfang Keep, Main only

  • Razorclaw the Butcher drops the Tanning Rack blueprint, the next Leatherworking camp object. Worth one run at 22 to 24.

Gnomeregan prep

  • At 20, pick up the Gnomeregan quests: Save Techbot's Brain! and Essential Artificials (Tinker Town), Gnogaine (Ozzie Togglevolt, Kharanos), and Gyrodrillmatic Excavationators (Shoni, Stormwind).
Phase 4: Add the support alts and prepare the Excavation Site Core four 25 to 29; support alts 1 to 14
GoalBring the five support alts online while the core four move toward 30.
You're done whenSupport alts are 14 with the Phase 2 quests in their logs. The core four have the Excavation Site quests.

Support alts

  • Level each to 6 or 7 and park. Then take them to 10, doing their starting-zone class quests.
  • Starting as a Dwarf or Human puts them closest to Hall of Thanes and Deadmines. A Night Elf follows the same Teldrassil trip as your Priest and Warrior.
  • At 14, pick up the same Phase 2 quests: the Hall of Thanes chain, the Westfall Deadmines chains and the Stormwind Deadmines quests.

Core four: 25 to 29

  • Ashenvale, then Thousand Needles (28 to 31). In Astranaar, pick up Seeking Caitlin from Llana, the reagent vendor.

Excavation Site: Wetlands (8 Alliance quests, against 4 for Horde)

  • Songblade Search (Dorin Songblade, Lakeshire), then Fallen in the Fen (inside).
  • Seeking Caitlin, then Lost in the Thicket Things (Caitlin Grassman, Menethil), then Heartwoven.
  • Highland Hides (James Halloran, Menethil, after Young Crocolisk Skins). This was bugged on the beta, so check it at launch.
  • Horrors in the Highland (Rethiel the Greenwarden, central Wetlands), after Tramping Paws, Fire Taboo and Blisters on the Land.
  • Lost Relic Carry, then Prehistoric Prism: the Titan Relic drops from the Relic Guardian (28%). Prospector Whelgar sends you to High Explorer Magellas in Ironforge.
  • Entrance: the hill southeast of Whelgar's Excavation Site.

Class quests while in the Wetlands

  • Warrior, Fire Hardened Armor: Scorched Spider Fangs from the Leech Stalkers and Cave Stalkers around /way 46 61.
  • Shaman at 30, Call of Air: Eldrun Stormbreaker in Ironforge sends you to Veyric Thunderhame in the northern hills of Arathi Highlands, one zone over.
Phase 5: City of Dalaran and the push to 35 Core four 28 to 35; support alts 14 to 25
GoalFinish the core four at 35, get professions to 225, and bring every support alt to 25.
You're done whenCore four are 35, support alts are 25, and you're at or near the 16-point cap.

City of Dalaran (levels 28 to 33)

  • Alliance needs no key. Talk to the Image of Archmage Modera outside the city, then enter through the Cantrips & Crows sewers.

More dungeons and zones toward 35

  • Gnomeregan (levels 25 to 38, 13 quests, most from Ironforge). The Horde route doesn't get this one.
  • Razorfen Kraul (levels 25 to 34): Mortality Wanes (inside) and The Crone of the Kraul (Feralas). The Shaman's Call of Air chain continues with Charlga Razorflank, the last boss.
  • Scarlet Monastery Graveyard and Library: Mythology of the Titans (Librarian Mae Paledust, Ironforge).
  • Quest zones: Hillsbrad (30 to 33), Desolace (31 to 34), or the new Riverglades (30 to 45, Farholde Keep flight path, reached from Redridge).

Professions to 225

  • The cheapest to push are Enchanting, Leatherworking, Alchemy and Tailoring. Each one that reaches 225 is another point.
  • Supply crates for the Azeroth Commerce Authority pay Merchant's Favor. The first 50 coins buy a recipe.

Support alts: gauntlet, then 25

  • Run Hall of Thanes, Deadmines and Wailing Caverns, then park at 20. With the Cozy Sleeping Bag you can park at a dungeon door and still build rested XP.
  • Take each to 25 through the Stockade and Blackfathom Deeps. The Hunter gets Engineering to 150 with the Warrior's ore and bars.

Where the Horde guide needs fixing

These apply to both factions. They come from Blizzard's Legacy announcement, the beta client data and the beta patch notes.

Level points are per class, not per characterBlizzard awards the level 25, 45 and 60 points once for each class. Nine characters only earn nine points if they are nine different classes, so the roster has one of each.
Gathering professions earn no pointsOnly Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Enchanting, Engineering, Leatherworking and Tailoring count, at 150, 225 and 300. Your core four cover five of these, so the Hunter takes Engineering for the sixth.
The original alt build is 21 pointsWell Rested 5, Talented 5, Bountiful Harvest 5, Thrill of Adventure 5, plus 1, adds up to 21. The cap is 16. The fixed builds are in the Legacy section.
Dungeon XP has changedOct 1: bonus XP from dungeon quests was cut by half. Oct 8: dungeon kills give 20% more XP. Rested XP only doubles kill XP, so entering a dungeon fully rested is worth more than before.
Legacy can open before level 25It unlocks the first time a character reaches 25, gets a crafting profession to 150, or explores the whole map. Getting Leatherworking or Tailoring to 150 early opens it sooner.
Changing perks costs 10 goldA class trainer resets your Legacy perks for 10g. Use Working Overtime while professions are climbing, then swap it out.

The 55-point mount

The Legacy reward track unlocks cosmetics at 15, 25, 40 and 55 points. The 55 reward is the Reins of the Spectral Bear. Blizzard hasn't said whether it's epic speed, so treat "free epic mount" as unconfirmed. Beyond the 45 points from leveling and crafting, there are 3 dungeon challenges (levels 15 to 25, 26 to 45 and 46 to 60), Explorer (1), the Lord Valthalak set quest (1), Hyjal Summit and Barrow Deeps (2), and up to 12 from PvP.
