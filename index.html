Route

Tap a step to tick it.
Phase 1: Bags first Warlock → Priest → Warrior → Shaman, each 1–10

Done when: everyone is 10 with 4 bags, parked.

1. Warlock (Northshire)

  • Enchanting + Tailoring.
  • 8 Linen Bags: keep 4, mail 4 to the Priest.
  • Disenchant every green. Class quests: Imp, Voidwalker.

2. Priest (Teldrassil)

  • Herbalism + Alchemy. Mail potions to every alt.
  • Mail Linen for tailoring to the Warlock.

Warlock: log in

  • 4 bags to the Warrior.

3. Warrior (Teldrassil)

  • Mining + Blacksmithing.
  • Mail Linen for tailoring and greens for disenchanting to the Warlock.

Warlock: log in

  • 4 bags to the Shaman.

4. Shaman (Dun Morogh)

  • Skinning + Leatherworking. Camp Tent at Leatherworking 20.
  • Mail greens for disenchanting to the Warlock.
  • Optional: create the support alts and park them.
Phase 2: First gauntlet Levels 13–21

Done when: core four are 20–21 and one craft is at 150.

Night Elves (Priest + Warrior) at ~11

  • Darkshore to 13, then boat to Stormwind and tram to Ironforge.

Grab the quests

Hall of Thanes

  • Old Ironforge Incursion · Earthseer Farsen, Gol'Bolar QuarryFirst: loot the Underground Map from Dark Iron Spies (Dun Morogh) and hand it to Farsen.Lead-in: A Visitor to Dun Morogh (Beldin Steelgrill, Steelgrill's Depot).
  • The Restless Dead · Afadra Dunwall, Old Ironforge entrance
  • Important Heirlooms · Thom Filch, Old Ironforge entrance

Inside: An Ancient Grudge, The Treaty of Understanding.

Deadmines

  • Destruction in Deadmines · Alba Fairmoon, Sentinel HillFirst: her chain from Testing the Wells, through Sprite Jumpsprocket and Jasper Fel in Stormwind. Leave by the rear exit for part 2.
  • The Defias Brotherhood · Gryan Stoutmantle, Sentinel HillFirst: the earlier parts of the chain (Stormwind, Redridge, Westfall).
  • Red Silk Bandanas · Scout Riell, Sentinel Hill towerFirst: unlocks partway through The Defias Brotherhood.
  • Collecting Memories, Oh Brother... · Wilder Thistlenettle, Stormwind Dwarven District
  • Underground Assault · Shoni the Shilent, Stormwind Dwarven DistrictLead-in: Speak with Shoni (Gnoarn, Tinker Town, Ironforge).

Inside: The Unsent Letter (VanCleef). Keep it: it starts the Stockade Riots chain.

Wailing Caverns

  • Deviate Hides, Deviate Eradication · Nalpak, cave above the entrance
  • Smart Drinks · Mebok Mizzyrix, RatchetFirst: Raptor Horns (Mebok).
  • Trouble at the Docks · Crane Operator Bigglefuzz, Ratchet

Inside: The Glowing Shard (Mutanus).

Run rested

  • Hall of Thanes → Deadmines → Wailing Caverns.
  • Cozy Sleeping Bag at 14: park anywhere.
  • Tailoring or Leatherworking to 150 first: it unlocks Legacy Points.
Phase 3: Stockade + Blackfathom Levels 21–25

Done when: core four are 25 (about 9 points).

Route

  • Darkshore → Redridge → Duskwood → Ashenvale.
  • Stockade, then Blackfathom Deeps.

Grab the quests

Stockade

  • What Comes Around... · Guard Berton, Lakeshire
  • Crime and Punishment · Councilman Millstipe, Darkshire
  • Quell the Uprising · Warden Thelwater, Stockade entrance
  • The Color of Blood · Nikova Raskol, Old Town
  • The Stockade Riots · Warden ThelwaterFirst: The Unsent Letter (Deadmines) → Baros Alexston → Bazil Thredd.

Blackfathom Deeps

  • Knowledge in the Deeps · Gerrig Bonegrip, Forlorn Cavern, Ironforge
  • Researching the Corruption · Gershala Nightwhisper, Auberdine
  • In Search of Thaelrid · Dawnwatcher Shaedlass, Darnassus
  • Twilight Falls · Argent Guard Manados, Darnassus
  • Warlock: The Orb of Soran'ruk · Doan Karhan, Barrens

Inside: Blackfathom Villainy (Thaelrid, after In Search of Thaelrid), The Heart of the Void (Aku'mai).

Class quests at 20

  • Shaman: Call of Water.
  • Priest: Elune's Grace.
  • Warrior: Fire Hardened Armor.
  • Warlock: Succubus, paired with Wailing Caverns.

  • Shaman at 30: Call of Air.

Inside: Heartwoven, Fallen in the Fen, Lost Relic Carry → Prehistoric Prism (High Explorer Magellas, Ironforge).

Phase 5: Push to 35 Core 28–35 · Support 14–25

Done when: core four are 35, support alts are 25, about 16 points.

  • Professions to 225.
  • Warrior: once the ore is banked, swap Mining for Engineering.

  • Gyrodrillmatic Excavationators · Shoni the Shilent, Stormwind Dwarven District

Inside: Willix the Importer (escort), Mortality Wanes.

Support alts

  • Phase 2 and 3 dungeons and quests, rested, to 25.

Legacy Point priority

  1. Early: Working Overtime. Faster profession leveling.
  2. After the profession push: Well Rested.

Alts 5–916 points

Well Rested 5, Talented 5, then Bountiful Harvest 5 (gatherers) or Thrill of Adventure 5 (zuggers), last 1 anywhere.

How many points

  • 18–19 once the core four and alts 5–9 are 25.
  • 16 spendable at launch.
  • 45+ long term, from 60s and professions.
  • 55 for the epic mount: about 10 more.

Check at launch

  • Novice Spelunker dungeon list unpublished; may need Ragefire Chasm.
  • Quest pickups come from beta data; a few chains may change.
