Route

Tap a step to tick it.
Phase 1: Supplies and bags Shaman → Warlock → Warrior → Priest, each 1–10

Done when: everyone is 10 with bags, parked.

1. Shaman (Dun Morogh)

  • Skinning + Mining. Mail copper to the Warrior.
  • Leaving Coldridge: drop Mining for Leatherworking. Camp Tent at Leatherworking 20.
  • Mail Linen for tailoring, greens for disenchanting and 5 silver to the Warlock.

2. Warlock (Northshire)

  • Enchanting + Tailoring.
  • Linen Bags: keep 4, mail the rest to the Shaman, Warrior and Priest.
  • Disenchant every green. Class quests: Imp, Voidwalker.

3. Warrior (Teldrassil)

  • Mining + Blacksmithing.
  • Mail Linen for tailoring and greens for disenchanting to the Warlock.

4. Priest (Teldrassil)

  • Herbalism + Alchemy. Mail potions to every alt.
  • Mail Linen for tailoring to the Warlock.
  • Optional: create the support alts and park them.
Phase 2: Prep Each 10–14

Done when: core four are 14 with the Hall of Thanes quests, parked.

Night Elves (Priest + Warrior) at 10

  • Darkshore to 12, then boat to Stormwind.

Shaman at 10

  • Thelsamar to 12, then tram to Stormwind.

Core four

  • Hogger (Elwynn), The People's Militia parts 1–2 (Westfall).
  • Easy Redridge quests to ~80% into 14.
  • Ironforge: Underground Map, then grab the Hall of Thanes quests. Park.
  • Cozy Sleeping Bag at 14: park anywhere.

Grab the quests

Hall of Thanes

  • Old Ironforge Incursion · Earthseer Farsen, Gol'Bolar QuarryFirst: loot the Underground Map from Dark Iron Spies (Dun Morogh) and hand it to Farsen.Lead-in: A Visitor to Dun Morogh (Beldin Steelgrill, Steelgrill's Depot).
  • The Restless Dead · Afadra Dunwall, Old Ironforge entrance
  • Important Heirlooms · Thom Filch, Old Ironforge entrance

Inside: An Ancient Grudge, The Treaty of Understanding.

Phase 3: Hall of Thanes + Defias chain Each 14–18

Done when: core four are 18 with every Deadmines quest, parked.

Core four, rested

  • Hall of Thanes: about a level and a half, to 16.
  • People's Militia part 3, then the Defias chain to the escort: 18. Fill with Darkshore, not Redridge.

Grab the quests

Deadmines

  • Destruction in Deadmines · Alba Fairmoon, Sentinel HillFirst: her chain from Testing the Wells, through Sprite Jumpsprocket and Jasper Fel in Stormwind. Leave by the rear exit for part 2.
  • The Defias Brotherhood · Gryan Stoutmantle, Sentinel HillFirst: The People's Militia, then the Lakeshire, Stormwind and Sentinel Hill runs, the Defias Messenger and the Defias Traitor escort.
  • Red Silk Bandanas · Scout Riell, Sentinel Hill towerFirst: unlocks partway through The Defias Brotherhood.
  • Collecting Memories, Oh Brother... · Wilder Thistlenettle, Stormwind Dwarven District
  • Underground Assault · Shoni the Shilent, Stormwind Dwarven DistrictLead-in: Speak with Shoni (Gnoarn, Tinker Town, Ironforge).
Phase 4: Deadmines Each 18–20

Done when: core four are 20, parked, and one craft is at 150.

Core four, rested

  • Deadmines with every quest. With a full group, farm Miners' Union Cards.
  • Keep The Unsent Letter (VanCleef): it starts the Stockade Riots chain.
  • Stormwind book deliveries, then Redridge (Yowler, murlocs) to 20. Park.
  • Tailoring or Leatherworking to 150 first: it unlocks Legacy Points.
Phase 5: Blackfathom prep Each 20–23

Done when: core four are 23 with every Blackfathom quest, parked.

Core four

  • Darkshore, then Redridge, to 23. Park.
  • Crafts to 225 from 20. Spare silver goes into professions.

Grab the quests

  • Twilight Falls · Argent Guard Manados, Darnassus
  • Warlock: The Orb of Soran'ruk · Doan Karhan, Barrens

Inside: Blackfathom Villainy (Thaelrid, after In Search of Thaelrid), The Heart of the Void (Aku'mai).

  • Warrior: Fire Hardened Armor.
  • Warlock: Succubus.

Legacy Point priority

  1. Early: Working Overtime. Faster profession leveling.
  2. After the profession push: Well Rested.

Alts 5–916 points

Well Rested 5, Talented 5, then Bountiful Harvest 5 (gatherers) or Thrill of Adventure 5 (zuggers), last 1 anywhere.

How many points

  • 18–19 once the core four and alts 5–9 are 25.
  • 16 spendable at launch.
  • 45+ long term, from 60s and professions.
  • 55 for the epic mount: about 10 more.
